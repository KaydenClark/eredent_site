# Kiros Map Description

Copied from the Google Doc of the same name (Azlemzyk/Maps). It records the geographic and named features shown
across the current Kiros map and its older map references. The newest clean outline and biome map define the present
coastlines, landmass arrangement, mountains, lakes, and terrain. Older maps supply names and historical context where
the same feature can still be identified.

## World Overview

Kiros is the principal mapped region of Azlemzyk. Most of the map lies in the northern hemisphere, with the equator
crossing only the southern portion. The page is read with north at the top for geographic description, regardless of
the decorative compass angle used on some versions.

Kiros is a lush garden world with enormous forests, abundant life, dragons, and creatures more dangerous than dragons.
The landscape is beautiful and heavily inhabited by wild life, but it is not broadly safe. The contrast between
protected inland waters and the dangerous outer ocean is one of the map's defining geographic and cultural features.

## Continental History and Water

The major landmasses were once joined in a Pangaea-like supercontinent. They have broken apart and continue to drift
away from one another. Water has filled the widening gaps, producing broad interior seas, flooded shelves, narrow
channels, and dense island chains between the surviving continental blocks.

The interior waters are shallow compared with the outer ocean. On terrain maps they appear as the lighter blue water
inside and between the major continents. The gods have acted to keep monsters out of these waters, making them
relatively safe to cross without the protection normally required at sea. Older lore described the divine action as
turning this water fresh, but the settled map fact is that the shallow water is protected and comparatively safe.

The outer ocean is deeper, darker, and more dangerous. It surrounds the broken continental system and contains the
kinds of creatures that make unprotected travel hazardous. The shallow seas therefore function as both geological scars
from continental separation and the main safe maritime corridors of Kiros.

## Major Landmasses

### Fiira

Fiira is the largest western continent in the current naming. It occupies most of the left side of the map and has a
long, broken eastern margin facing the interior shallow seas. Its northern and northwestern country is cold to
temperate and heavily forested, not desert. Mixed forest dominates around the northwestern mountains, with colder forest
farther north and at higher elevations.

A dominant north-south mountain system runs through the continent. The central portion is the Veir Mountains, a tall,
rugged, continuous range comparable in form to the Rocky Mountains. The range encloses and defines the Lake Veir basin.
Western and central uplands descend toward the fragmented eastern coast and the shallow sea. Warmer forest and tropical
country become more common toward the south, while arid terrain is confined to lower southern areas rather than the
cold north.

Older maps also use Rongle or Roglie as a place name within the western land, but Rongle is not the continent. Its
exact modern scope is not yet defined in this description.

### Ender

Ender is the far-western island or detached landmass beside Fiira. It is mostly low to moderate in elevation and is
defined more by its ecological transition than by a major alpine range. The upper portion is temperate mixed forest.
The lower portion is tropical, hot, and densely vegetated. Ener is the principal named settlement on Ender.

### Roan

Roan is the large northeastern continent. Its broad northern interior is forested, with cold forest and taiga across
the far north and mixed forest farther south. Lower coastal margins face the interior shallow seas, while the outer
northern and eastern coasts face deeper water.

Roan's principal mountains form a fragmented southern-central system rather than one uninterrupted wall. The ranges
resemble the mountain provinces of California, with separate blocks and volcanic origins. Volcanic activity helped
create the ranges, although the map does not identify individual volcanoes there. Sfere, one of the three major lakes,
lies near these mountains. Arris is the principal currently confirmed city near Sfere.

### Create and the Central Break Zone

Create is the largest surviving landmass directly below Roan within the central continental break zone. It is
surrounded by branching shallow channels and neighboring islands. Its coastline is exceptionally complex because it
occupies the partially drowned seam between separating continents. Create is primarily forested lowland and moderate
upland, with scattered smaller ridges rather than a continent-dominating mountain chain.

The many islands and peninsulas around Create are remnants of the same breakup. Together they form stepping-stone land
routes through the shallow seas and help divide the protected interior waters into smaller basins and passages.

### Malos

Malos is the large southeastern continent. It is older and flatter overall than Fiira or Roan. The western margin is
highly fragmented into drowned coasts, island mazes, and shallow channels. The broad interior is lowland, and the
continent's principal relief comes from the Malos Spine along the east and southeast.

The Malos Spine is the oldest major range on the map. Its mountains are worn, rounded, and lower than the Veir
Mountains, comparable to the Appalachians. East of the Spine lies flat, humid, near-sea-level country comparable to
Florida and Louisiana, with swamps, wetlands, bayous, and flood-prone coastal lowlands.

Dire Lake lies in a large depressed basin within Malos. The center of the lake basin is below present sea level, and
the map shows a distinct inner depression within the larger lake. Noc is a confirmed settlement in the western or
interior portion of Malos, and Diron is associated with the Dire Lake area on older maps.

### Dragon Islands

The Dragon Islands form the spiral-shaped southern archipelago below the western and central continents. They are
volcanic. The main central island contains one dominant volcano, while the smaller surrounding islands are volcanic
remnants and broken extensions of the same island system. The archipelago sits in warmer southern water near the
equatorial portion of the map.

### Northern Islands

A long belt of islands spans the northern edge of the map. These islands are colder, rocky, and wind-exposed. Several
contain mountain ridges, and the northernmost or highest grey-marked mountains carry snow. Older maps identify island
groups in this belt as the Icaia or Icala Islands and the Jerr Islands. The exact modern boundaries of those island
groups are not yet fixed.

## Named Water Features

### Major Lakes

- Lake Veir lies within the Veir mountain country on Fiira. It occupies a large enclosed or partly enclosed high basin,
  similar in concept to ancient Lake Bonneville within the Rocky Mountain region.
- Sfere lies on Roan near the fragmented southern mountain system. Arris is located beside or immediately east of it in
  the current placement notes.
- Dire Lake lies on Malos in a broad low basin. Its inner depression marks the deepest part of a basin whose center is
  below sea level.

### Named Seas and Oceans on Older Maps

Older labeled maps name several surrounding waters. Their coastlines remain useful references, although some names may
later be revised as the new map is labeled.

- North Ocean, the deep water along the northern edge.
- Orc Ocean, the deep water beyond the southern and southeastern edge of Malos.
- Ice Sea, the cold northern water among and below the northern islands.
- Dragon Sea, the water surrounding the Dragon Islands and the southern central passages.
- Elif Sea, an older name for part of the protected central water between the western lands and Roan.
- Thiefs Sea, an older name for a channel or sea east of Create and west of Malos.
- Ender Ocean and Places of Origin, older labels appearing near the outer southern waters on historical maps.

## Terrain and Climate

### Forests

Forests are the dominant biome across Kiros. They are immense, ancient, and dense enough to cover large portions of
continents rather than appearing as isolated woodland patches. Temperate mixed forest covers much of Fiira, Create, and
the middle latitudes. Cold evergreen forest and taiga dominate northern Roan, the northern part of Fiira, and colder
islands. Tropical and hot forest occupy southern Ender, the warmer southern coasts, and other low-latitude regions.

The world remains lush even outside the tropical belt. Mixed forest has the scale and density of a primeval version of
Yellowstone rather than ordinary sparse woodland. Tropical forest is hotter, wetter, and more jungle-like. Cold forest
is darker and more evergreen-dominated.

### Deserts and Drylands

Desert is concentrated in the warmer southern and southwestern parts of the map. It does not occupy the northern half
of Fiira or the upper part of Ender. The older maps show dry regions near Ignas and portions of the southern western
land, while the current biome map retains arid country mainly in the far south.

### Wetlands

The largest wetland identity belongs to the low country east of the Malos Spine and to the drowned coastal margins of
Malos. These regions are flat, humid, and nearly at sea level, with swamp, marsh, bayou, and shallow-water
transitions. Smaller wetlands can also occur in the central break zone where low land meets the protected shallow
seas.

### Snow and Cold Regions

Snow is limited to the far north and to the highest mountain elevations. Grey-marked northern peaks and island ridges
are snow-covered. The Veir Mountains can retain snow on their highest or northern summits, while the older, lower Malos
Spine is generally not a major snow range.

## Mountain Systems

- Veir Mountains: the dominant Rockies-like range on Fiira, long, rugged, high, and strongly connected. Lake Veir is
  embedded within its mountain basin.
- Roan ranges: fragmented mountain blocks of volcanic origin near the southern part of Roan and around Sfere. The
  ranges are discontinuous and do not contain a specifically marked volcano.
- Malos Spine: the oldest mountain system, a long Appalachian-like backbone of rounded and heavily weathered mountains
  along eastern and southeastern Malos.
- Dragon Islands volcanic massif: a central dominant volcano surrounded by smaller volcanic islands and remnants.
- Northern island ridges: smaller cold ranges, some with permanent or seasonal snow.

Older maps also name the Goblin Mountains on a northern island, the Nuclan Mountains in the western mountain country,
and the Malos Mountains along the southeastern continent. These names may refer to subdivisions of the present larger
systems.

### Other Names Preserved by Older Maps

Historical maps contain additional names whose modern locations or spellings have not been fully reconciled with the
new outline. They remain part of the map record rather than confirmed current labels.

- Core, Capet, Terra, Fama, Hima, Ignas, Galem, Bose or Boge, Diron, Oreis or Oasis, Amel, Forner or Farner, Roglie,
  and Drognor.
- Icaia or Icala Islands, Jerr Islands, Dice Islands, Onion Islands, Saphaun Islands, and other unnamed northern and
  southern island groups.
- Older route maps also show territorial boundaries, trade routes, and an Angel City or divine city. These are
  political or lore layers, not fixed physical features of the current clean map.

## Current Map Scope

The current map records coastlines, major islands, mountain systems, major lakes, shallow and deep water, forest zones,
deserts, wetlands, and cold or snowy regions. Rivers are not currently mapped at world scale. City labels, political
borders, trade routes, and the Angel City are also absent from the clean geographic version, although their names and
older placements remain documented above.

The defining image of Kiros is a broken former supercontinent surrounded by dangerous deep ocean and divided internally
by pale protected seas. Continuous young mountains dominate Fiira, fragmented volcanic ranges shape Roan, worn ancient
mountains border the lowlands of Malos, and the Dragon Islands rise from a volcanic spiral in the south. Across all of
it, immense forests and dangerous life make Kiros a garden world rather than a tame one.
