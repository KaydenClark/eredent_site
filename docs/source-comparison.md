# Source comparison: kiros_earth_re vs the penup master

Generated by `python pipeline/compare_sources.py` (2026-10-10). All numbers are also in
`public/data/source_comparison.json`. Nothing here changes the terrain: **the 3D world is still built from the
September penup master** (`pipeline/source/penup_20260912_182533.jpg`). No original image was modified (the script
checks md5 before and after).

## Method

1. **Frame test.** `kiros_earth_re.png` and `kiros_v5.png` are the clean coastline scan
   `Copy of saphaun_drawn_Empty.jpg`, shrunk to exactly half size, with marks drawn on top. 99.35% of earth_re's pixels
   are within 4 grey levels of the shrunk scan, and every scan ink pixel sits within 1 px of earth_re ink. earth_re and
   v5 have 90.75% identical pixels. `saphaun_drawn_edit.jpg` is the same scan at full size plus its own marks (98.7%
   of pixels match). So one registration covers all of them. Whatever differs from the clean scan is the new drawing:
   blue marks are lakes, everything else is brown mountain marks.
2. **Registration onto the master grid.** The script matches earth_re's coastline ink to the penup ink by trimmed
   iterative closest point: it starts from the bounding boxes, shrinks the match gate from 40 px to 2.5 px, fits an
   affine and then a homography. The homography wins:
   - **97.2% inliers** (41,815 of 43,008 coastline pixels land within 2 px of penup ink; 85.6% within 1 px).
   - **RMS 0.83 master px** (about 1.9 km on the small world). Scale 1.121, rotation -0.01°.
   - As a check, v5's own lakes measured through the same transform land within 2 to 5 km of the penup lakes, with
     areas within 1 to 4% of them.
   - The pipeline's `register.py` (ORB + RANSAC; output in `pipeline/build/biome_reg.png`) fits slightly worse.
     Its v5 dark ink lies within 2 px of the penup coast 85.8% of the time; with this registration the figure is 91.0%.
3. **Penup classes.** Blue ink is lakes (the rule from `masks.py`). Grey ink is split into coast and mountain marks
   with the `masks.py` land/water parity rule, plus two filters: a mark must be a separate open stroke, and it must lie
   at least 6 px (median) from the coast. Without these, pieces of the fractal Malos archipelago were counted as marks.
4. **Lakes.** Each closed blue loop becomes a lake whose edge is the centre of the ink line. Small gaps in a loop are
   bridged. Areas are true areas on the sphere (the azimuthal-equidistant area factor is applied), on the small world
   (R 1692.7 km, 2.2453 km per master px) and on the Earth-sized world (R 6371 km, so lengths ×3.764 and areas ×14.17).
   Outlines are simplified to at most 80 points and stored in lat/lon.

- **Penup centroids:** Veir 45.9°N 27.9°W; Sfere 47.5°N 21.8°E; Dire 15.2°N 32.7°E.
- **earth_re centroids:** Veir 32.1°N 21.2°W; Sfere 46.6°N 23.6°E; Dire 13.7°N 30.1°E.
- **earth_re shapes.** Veir is about 150 × 70 km (small world) with its long axis running NNW–SSE. Dire is a narrow
  sliver, about 190 × 50 km, lying inside the western half of the old Dire outline. Sfere is a tiny figure-8 (two small
  loops drawn together) on the south-east edge of the old Sfere.
- **Uncertainty.** Moving the line centre by half a pixel changes the areas by about ±2–4% for penup, ±6–8% for the
  earth_re Veir and Dire, and about ±20% for the earth_re Sfere, which is only about 7 × 7 master px.
- **Older figures.** `pipeline/config.json` lists Lake Veir as 51,000 km² (small) and 722,000 km² (Earth), and
  `docs/kiros_earth_scale.png` labels it 690k km². This method measures 44,700 km² true area, or 45,900 km² with the
  flat ruler. The older figures probably measured to the outside of the ink line.

## Mountain marks

Stroke counts are per range box in `config.json`, the same boxes `terrain.py` uses, and a stroke counts in every box
that contains it. Lengths are approximate, on the small world.

| Box | Penup strokes (≈km) | earth_re strokes (≈km) |
|---|---|---|
| veir | 65 (4,170) | 12 (1,280) |
| nw_fiira | 7 (430) | 1 (270), and the line runs on past the box into the veir box |
| north_isle | 5 (400) | **0** |
| ne_isles | 4 (250) | **0** |
| roan | 14 (1,120) | 8 (1,150) |
| malos | 23 (2,730) | 14 (1,820) |
| dragon | 7 (950) | 2 (460) |
| fiira_s | 8 (710) | 3 (410) |
| total | 120 strokes (≈9,600 km) | 37 strokes (≈5,060 km) |

- **Veir ring.** Penup has marks in all 16 of 16 compass directions within 120 px of its Lake Veir, a full ring.
  earth_re has marks in only 5 (E, ESE, SW, WSW, W). Its Veir range is one NNE–SSW zigzag line that passes right by
  the new lake: the northern half lies to the lake's NE and the southern half to its SW. The new lake sits in the
  southern arm of the old ring belt: penup marks surround the new lake's position in 14 of 16 directions. Penup also has 3 small marks drawn inside its Lake Veir;
  earth_re has none.
- **NW Fiira.** Both sources have a NW–SE line. earth_re's line is longer, reaching about master px (405, 420), past
  the `nw_fiira` box. With the current boxes, its south-east end would be lifted as part of the Veir range.
- **Roan.** earth_re has one long range across the north of Roan, plus one SW–NE line just south of Sfere. Penup has
  a SW–NE line south and east of Sfere, a short mark group in the southern lobe, and marks on small islands. Penup
  draws no northern Roan range.
- **Malos.** Both have a spine along the east coast that turns west along the south coast. earth_re's strokes are
  fewer and wider spaced. earth_re also has one short isolated stroke at the east coast.
- **Northern islands.** The north isle and the NE "snow" isles have no marks at all in earth_re.
- **Dragon isle.** earth_re has one compact mark group (2 strokes) on the south-west part of the isle, where penup has 7
  zigzags.

## Map A rotation

**Verdict: north points to the image's right. Rotating 90° counter-clockwise gives north up.** This was checked
visually against the master. After the rotation:

- the long Ender island is in the west;
- the continent with a lake inside crossing ranges (Fiira and Veir) is west-centre;
- the northern continent with a lake (Roan and Sfere) is north-east;
- the crescent continent with a heart-shaped lake and a spine of marks along its outer edge (Malos and Dire) is
  south-east;
- the hatched round isle (Dragon) is south-west.

A coarse ink-density correlation agrees, but only weakly: +0.078 for counter-clockwise against -0.074 for clockwise.
The rotated copy is `public/sources/derived/kiros_map_a_northup.jpg`. Map A's coastlines are older and are not
registered.

## Outputs for the app

- `public/sources/derived/penup_lines_master.png` and `earth_re_lines_master.png`: RGBA ink on the master grid. Lakes
  are #2f6fff, marks #b8742a, coast #5a5a5a.
- `public/sources/derived/v5_on_master.png`: the full-colour biome map registered to the master grid, transparent
  outside its frame.
- `public/textures/overlay_penup_window.png` and `overlay_earth_re_window.png`:
  - 4096×2478 equirectangular images covering lon -84..78 and lat -14..84, with row 0 at 84°N.
  - Sample them with `uv = ((lon+84)/162, (lat+14)/98)`.
  - Lines are at least about 2 px wide. Lakes are filled at 35% alpha, and penup's Dire inner depression gets a second
    35% layer.
  - The penup overlay lines up with the existing `kiros_region_coast_4k.png` shoreline.
- `public/sources/previews/*.jpg` (at most 1600 px) and `public/sources/thumbs/*.jpg` (360 px): one per unique image.
  `source_comparison.json` → `files` lists every original path, its md5, its frame, the copy it duplicates and a
  description.

## Caveats

- earth_re is drawn on the 1208×954 image with lines about 2 px wide, so its lake outlines are only good to about
  ±1 source px (about 2.5 km on the small world). The tiny Sfere loop is the least certain.
- Registration error (0.83 px RMS) is negligible next to the shifts above.
- Stroke lengths are perimeter estimates. Landmass names come from the `config.json` region seeds. The Roan and Malos
  boxes overlap, so a stroke in the overlap counts in both, just as `terrain.py` would treat it.
- All km figures depend on the open planet-size choice.
