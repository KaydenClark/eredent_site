Kiros, before the first people

Twenty years of a world, as it stands in the sources tonight: walk it, fly it, and question it. The ground is built from Kayden's September penup outline and biome map; the newest drawing and every older map are in the workshop.

Start somewhere

Trees, rocks, sound and the relief finer than about 1 km are generated stand-ins, not canon. No towns, roads, labels or people anywhere in the world.

Moving around Kiros

Survey M

Drag to move the ground. Wheel or pinch to descend. Right-drag or Shift-drag to turn and tilt. Keys: WASD/arrows pan, +/− height, Q/E turn, R/F tilt.

Walk G

Lands you where the middle of the screen points. W A S D walk, arrows turn, Shift run, Space jump (the small world has about a quarter of Earth's gravity). Wading slows you down.

Fly V

W A S D along the view, Space/E up, C/Q down, Shift boost. You go faster the higher you are.

Looking and speed

Click the view to capture the mouse; Esc releases it. Without capture, drag to look. Wheel or [ ] change speed. Touch screens get an on-screen pad; P shows it anywhere.

Anywhere

Home or Reset returns to orbit. L cycles the light: sunrise, midday, golden hour, a two-moon night. J opens the workshop: sources, comparisons, notes and bookmarks; B bookmarks the view you are looking at.

The ground is the September penup master. Trees, rocks and the fine relief below about 1 km are generated, not drawn by Kayden. No towns, roads, labels or people anywhere.
